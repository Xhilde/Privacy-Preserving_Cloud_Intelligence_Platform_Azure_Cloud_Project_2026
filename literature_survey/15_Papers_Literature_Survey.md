# Literature Survey — 15 Papers (2023–2026)
Privacy-Preserving Cloud Intelligence Platform using Federated Learning — Applied to Insider Threat Detection

1. Ye, X., Luo, F., Cui, H., et al. (2025). Research on insider threat detection based on personalized federated learning and behavior log analysis. Scientific Reports, 15, Article 19214. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-04029-w
   Relevance: Personalized FL applied directly to insider threat/behavior-log detection.

2. Gupta, K., Saxena, D., Gupta, R., Kumar, J., & Singh, A. K. (2024). FedMUP: Federated learning driven malicious user prediction model for secure data distribution in cloud environments. Applied Soft Computing, 157, Article 111519. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asoc.2024.111519
   Relevance: Uses the same CMU CERT r4.2 dataset; shares only computed risk values via federated averaging.

3. Kamatchi, K., & Uma, E. (2025). Securing the edge: Privacy-preserving federated learning for insider threats in IoT networks. The Journal of Supercomputing, 81, Article 246. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11227-024-06752-z
   Relevance: Combines privacy preservation with FL for insider threat detection.

4. Bunko, T., Johnstone, M. N., Yang, W., & Scott, B. A. (2026). A survey of privacy-preserving federated learning for intrusion detection systems. Artificial Intelligence Review, 59(5), Article 125. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-026-11519-4
   Relevance: Identifies that most FL-IDS work relies on data locality alone, not real privacy mechanisms.

5. Han, Q., Lu, S., Wang, W., Qu, H., Li, J., & Gao, Y. (2024). Privacy preserving and secure robust federated learning: A survey. Concurrency and Computation: Practice and Experience, 36. https://doi.org/10.1002/cpe.8084
   Relevance: Broad survey of privacy and robustness mechanisms applicable to aggregation.

6. Sharif, M. S., & Elmedany, W. (2024). Federated learning with hybrid differential privacy for secure and reliable cross-IoT platform knowledge sharing. Security and Privacy, 7(3), Article e374. https://doi.org/10.1002/spy2.374
   Relevance: Hybrid differential privacy design pattern reusable for this project's aggregation layer.

7. El Khediri, S., Zidi, S., & Rizwan, M. (2023). Privacy-preserving federated learning cyber-threat detection for intelligent transport systems with blockchain-based security. Expert Systems, 40(5), Article e13103.
   Relevance: Applied cross-domain example of FL plus privacy plus threat detection.

8. Yazdinejad, A., Dehghantanha, A., Srivastava, G., Karimipour, H., & Parizi, R. M. (2024). Hybrid privacy preserving federated learning against irregular users in next-generation internet of things. Journal of Systems Architecture, 148, Article 103088. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sysarc.2024.103088
   Relevance: Addresses anomalous client behavior within the FL process itself.

9. Vyas, A., Lin, P. C., Hwang, R. H., et al. (2024). Privacy-preserving federated learning for intrusion detection in IoT environments: A survey. IEEE Access, 12, 127018-127050. https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3454211
   Relevance: Survey of PPFL-IDS techniques usable as a technique menu.

10. Azeez, S. D., Ilyas, M., & Bako, I. M. (2024). Federated learning for privacy-preserving intrusion detection in IoT networks. In 2024 International Congress on Human-Computer Interaction, Optimization and Robotic Applications (HORA) (pp. 1-7). IEEE. https://doi.org/10.1109/HORA61326.2024.10550685
    Relevance: Practical implementation reference for a lightweight FL-IDS pipeline.

11. Hernandez-Ramos, J., Karopoulos, G., Chatzoglou, E., et al. (2025). Intrusion detection based on federated learning: A systematic review. ACM Computing Surveys. https://doi.org/10.1145/3731596
    Relevance: Recent systematic review cataloging FL-IDS architectures and open challenges.

12. Belenguer, A., Pascual, J. A., & Navaridas, J. (2025). A review of federated learning applications in intrusion detection systems. Computer Networks, 258, Article 111023. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2024.111023
    Relevance: Distinguishes deployment contexts relevant to this project's architecture choice.

13. Ratnayake, H., Chen, L., & Ding, X. (2023). A review of federated learning: Taxonomy, privacy and future directions. Journal of Intelligent Information Systems, 61, 923-949. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10844-023-00797-x
    Relevance: Classifies privacy-preservation techniques justifying this project's differential-privacy design.

14. Sun, S., Zhou, L., Wang, Z., et al. (2025). Robust intrusion detection based on personalized federated learning for IoT environment. Computers & Security, 154, Article 104442. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cose.2025.104442
    Relevance: Personalized aggregation addressing non-uniform client data.

15. Ugli, A. A. A., & Shin, J. S. (2025). SAFE-IDS: A privacy-preserving framework for overcoming Non-IID challenges in federated intrusion detection. Computers & Security, 155, Article 104492. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cose.2025.104492
    Relevance: Directly tackles the Non-IID problem this project faces across simulated departments.
